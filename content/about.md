## [Hello there!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rEq1Z0bjdwc)

{{< polaroid src="/me2_resized.jpg" alt="Jakub waving" caption="Hi! I’m Jakub 👋" max="280px" >}}

My name is **Jakub Duchniewicz** and this is my personal space :grin:

I work close to the hardware/software boundary: embedded systems, low-level programming, telecom, FPGAs, Rust, Linux, game engines and other things that tend to break in interesting ways.

I recently finished my contract at **[Trustworthy Systems]** at UNSW in Sydney where I was tasked with developing new drivers and components for the **[sDDF]** tooling based on **[seL4]** microkernel.

Previously, I have worked as a programmer at **[Tietoevry]**, doing **5G NR L1** development from the Modem UE side on custom ASIC platforms for an **Unspecified Client**. I am also a co-founder of **[Sticky Piston Studios]** — a jack-of-all-trades company where **we make any advanced tech tick**!

My usual playground is low-level programming in **C**, **C++** and **Rust**. I have worked with hardware accelerators such as **FPGA**s, so I happen to know some **Verilog** and **SystemVerilog** as well. Recently I have also been spending more time around **Rust game engines**, **hot reloading**, **ECS-style architecture**, microkernel-ish systems experiments and the kind of debugging sessions where hardware, software and reality disagree with each other.

Being a hardcore **gamejammer**, I am familiar with **Unity**, custom game engines and other popular tools of the trade. Some of that energy now goes into **[Pill Engine]**, **[PolyEngine]** and other experiments around building games with fewer black boxes.

In my free time I am doing a ton of **bouldering**, chasing them good waves :wave: and powder days :skiing:. When I am finally so tired that I can sit patiently in one place, I have time and energy for **hardware hacking** and some **reverse engineering** — preferably in the retro-computing space. I am also a dog-person, although right now because of my travels I do not have a doggo of my own.
