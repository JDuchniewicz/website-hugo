<section class="jk-about-panel">
<p class="jk-card-topline">What I do</p>
<h2>Close to the metal</h2>
<p>Professionally, I have worked on <strong>5G NR L1</strong> development from the Modem UE side on custom ASIC platforms at <strong><a href="https://www.tietoevry.com/">Tietoevry</a></strong>. I am also a co-founder of <strong><a href="https://stickypistonstudios.com/">Sticky Piston Studios</a></strong> — a jack-of-all-trades company where <strong>we make any advanced tech tick</strong>.</p>
<p>My usual playground is low-level programming in <strong>C</strong>, <strong>C++</strong> and <strong>Rust</strong>. I have worked with <strong>FPGAs</strong>, Verilog and SystemVerilog, and lately spend a lot of time around Rust game engines, hot reloading, ECS-style architecture and microkernel-ish systems experiments.</p>
</section>
<section class="jk-about-panel jk-about-panel-personal">
<p class="jk-card-topline">Outside work</p>
<h2>Waves, rocks & weird hardware</h2>
<p>I’m a hardcore <strong>gamejammer</strong>, and some of that energy goes into <strong><a href="https://github.com/MattSzymonski/Pill-Engine">Pill Engine</a></strong>, <strong><a href="https://github.com/PolyEngineTeam/PolyEngine/">PolyEngine</a></strong> and experiments around making games with fewer black boxes.</p>
<p>When I’m not staring at a debugger, I’m usually <strong>bouldering</strong>, chasing good waves 🌊 or powder days ⛷️. Quieter time tends to turn into hardware hacking, reverse engineering and retro-computing.</p>
</section>
